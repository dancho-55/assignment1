About Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. He is an Argentine professional football player. Messi spent most of his career at FC Barcelona and won many major trophies. He also helped Argentina win the FIFA World Cup in 2022.

Important Events

  1. 1987 - Born in Rosario, Argentina
  2. 2004 - Made his first-team debut for Barcelona
  3. 2009 - Won his first Ballon d'Or
  4. 2021 - Won Copa America with Argentina
  5. 2022 - Won the FIFA World Cup

Achievements

  • FIFA World Cup winner
  • Copa America winner
  • Multiple Ballon d'Or awards
  • Multiple UEFA Champions League titles
