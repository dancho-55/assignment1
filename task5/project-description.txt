Computer Builder Website

The Computer Builder Website helps users build their own computers by selecting compatible components.
 Users can choose parts such as CPU, GPU, RAM, motherboard, and storage. The website also provides pre-built PC options for different needs and budgets. 
 The target users are gamers, students, professionals, and anyone interested in building a PC.
